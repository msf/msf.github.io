A joint talk with [Jonas Irgens Kylling](https://github.com/jirgens) at [Trino Fest 2023](https://trino.io/blog/2023/07/14/trino-fest-2023-dune.html) (virtual, June 2023) on **DuneSQL** — Dune's query engine for blockchain data, built on Trino with native `INT256` / `UINT256` support. We covered the challenges of querying blockchain data, the move to Trino, and how DuneSQL is operated in production.
